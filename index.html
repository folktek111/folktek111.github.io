Technology is any essential technique or tool. Some of it is made of steel. Most of it is made of hands, wood, fungi and people talking.

  • Hands

    Traditional craft and building: windows, log houses, stoves, roofs, boats.

  • Nature

    Mushrooms, trees, soil and whole living systems doing the work.

  • People

    Circles, meetings and talking together, the oldest social tools there are.

Julian Baw

Craftsman, pedagogue and project leader based in Uddebo, in Tranemo kommun. British-born, Swedish citizen, working in English and Swedish.

I work on old and unusual buildings: restoring what is there, building what is needed, and teaching the skills along the way.

Folktekologi

An ongoing research strand into the simplest technologies people can use at home: low-impact, no or low voltage, working with natural systems rather than replacing them, and run through local cottage-industry economies and co-ops.
