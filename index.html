Folktek

Folktek is a long-term research project, many years in the making. It is a study to really analyse what technology is, and what we need as people to live in a resilient, simple, low-impact way: technologies that anyone can build and develop.

Four principles guide the work:

  • Low-impact: the smallest footprint in materials, energy and ecology.
  • Simplest first: no electricity where possible; add complexity only when the simple way fails.
  • Doable at home: buildable, repairable and understandable with local materials.
  • Open and reusable: shared knowledge, old computers, repairable tools.

What is technology?

We tend to picture technology as something advanced and highly intellectual: screens and circuits, made by experts, owned by companies, and impossible to fix yourself.

But the word comes from the Greek tekhnē: craft, skill, the art of making. A sharpened stick is technology. So is a clay oven, a woven basket, a waterwheel, a seed saved from last year's harvest, or a circle of people deciding something together. These are the technologies Folktek is looking for.
