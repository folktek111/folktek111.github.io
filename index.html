Folktek

Folktek is a long-term research project, worked on for many years, to identify the technologies people need to live in a resilient, simple, low-impact way: technologies that anyone can build and develop.

What is technology?

We tend to picture technology as something advanced and highly intellectual: screens and circuits, made by experts, owned by companies, and impossible to fix yourself.

But the word comes from the Greek tekhnē: craft, skill, the art of making. A sharpened stick is technology. So is a clay oven, a woven basket, a waterwheel, a seed saved from last year's harvest, or a circle of people deciding something together.

Folktek is a study into what technology really is, and a search for technologies that are simpler, more basic, more rudimentary, and accessible to all people everywhere: things you can understand, build, repair and share with whatever is at hand.
