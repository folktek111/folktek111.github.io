How to use it

Sweep the phone slowly over the floor until a ring appears. Points land where the ring is. Textured surfaces work best; plain white floors, walls and glass are hard for the phone to see.

Main buttons

Add point. Puts a point where the ring is.
Undo. Takes back the last thing you did.
Clear all. Removes everything and starts over.
More. Opens the tools below and the units.

Tools (in More)

Line (the default). Two taps make one A-to-B measurement. The next tap starts a new line.
Shape. Tap the corners one after another. The lines join up in a chain.
Height. For the top of something that is up in the air, like a door frame or a ceiling edge. Tap ＋ on the floor right below it, aim the phone at the top, tap ＋ to lock. Tap ＋ again to measure again.
Circle. Tap 3 points on the edge of something round. You get the diameter, the distance around, and the area.

Shape buttons

Close shape. After 3 or more corners: joins the last corner back to the first. Area shows the m² inside.
Flatten. Lines all the corners up on one flat plane (a floor, a wall) and shows it as a see-through surface. Undo puts them back.
Volume. Closed shape area × the last height you measured, in m³. Drawn as a box. Measure a height first with the Height tool.
m
Units. Switches between m / cm and mm.
Move slowly and keep the phone steady for the best results.
