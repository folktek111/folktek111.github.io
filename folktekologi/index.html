Folktekologi

The simplest technologies people can use at home: to grow food, build soil, make diversity and live on the land.

Technology is any essential technique or tool: mechanical, social, biological, digital. The Earth is a technology too, so advanced it is the medium itself. Plants and fungi are technologies; the work is learning to use them well.

  • Low-impact
  • Simplest first, no voltage where possible
  • Rewilding
  • Doable at home
  • Embrace what exists
  • Open and reusable
  • Local economies
  • Within current law
  • Communication is technology
